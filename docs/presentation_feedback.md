Feedback Received from Other Groups

During our project pitch, we received feedback from other groups that our AI Scam Message Detector should focus more on follow-up actions and recommendations, rather than only identifying whether a message is a scam. 
Based on this feedback, we plan to improve the system by providing users with clear and practical next steps according to the assessed risk level.

The system will include an action severity level based on risk. Low-risk messages will encourage users to verify the message through official channels. 
Medium-risk messages will advise users not to click links, reply, or provide personal information until the sender is independently verified. 
High-risk messages will provide urgent warnings and recommend actions such as blocking the sender, securing accounts, and contacting the relevant organisation.

We will also include safe verification recommendations, emphasising that users should never verify a suspicious message using contact details or links provided within the message. 
Instead, they should use the organisation's official website, application, or contact information. e.g. call the bank hotline to verify this message

A future addition could be to seperate our recommendations will be separated into two sections: “Before You Act”, which covers steps to take before clicking, replying, or transferring money, and “If You’ve Already Acted”, 
covering steps such as contacting a bank, securing affected accounts, changing password, and reporting the incident through the appropriate channels.

This feedback will help make our system more actionable and useful to users beyond simply detecting scams.
